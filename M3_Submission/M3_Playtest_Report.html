Monday: Playtesting Report (Milestone 3)

CS 319 Introduction to Game Design and Development, Habib University, Fall 2026. Team: Ayaan, Qasim, Yusra

1. Approach and target areas

Monday is a first-person slice-of-life game set in one bedroom: the player turns off an alarm, gets up and brushes their teeth. Ten players took part in observed sessions, above the minimum of 6 for a team of 3. Observers took notes on behaviour, confusion and bugs, and 9 of the 10 players submitted a survey. Unity Analytics sessions were recorded as a third source.

6. Analysis and planned modifications

Meets target. For an early build the target was a mean of at least 3.0 per item. Six of 7 items met it. Movement, drowsiness and wanting to keep playing were each rated 4 or 5 by 8 of 9 players, and 2 of 10 players praised the visual style. Needs refinement. Discoverability is the main weakness: object clarity was the only item below target (2.89), with 4 of 9 rating it 2 or below, and 6 of 9 needed help with the alarm. Two items that met the target were still rated 3 or lower by several players: picking up objects (4 of 9) and understanding the minigame (6 of 9). Completion does not measure engagement, and 4 of 9 found the exit rule unclear. Survey and notes differ on the alarm (6 of 9 against 4 of 10), possibly because observers noted only visible hesitation.
